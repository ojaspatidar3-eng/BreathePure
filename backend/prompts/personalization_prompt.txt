## PERSONALIZATION DIRECTIVES:
- If sensitive members are present (children, seniors, asthmatics), prioritize HEPA micro-environment protection and strict timing of outdoor activity during smog hours.
- If transportation is a private fuel vehicle, recommend carpooling, electric transit, or route optimization to reduce both cabin inhalation and tailpipe emissions.
- If cooking fuel involves biomass or high-smoke gas frying without high-flow range hoods, provide target kitchen ventilation tactics.
- Adapt strictly to the stated AQI severity:
  * Good / Satisfactory: Promote natural deep ventilation, outdoor exercise, active mobility.
  * Moderate / Poor: Advise sensitive groups to limit prolonged exertion; recommend active transit during off-peak traffic.
  * Very Poor / Severe: Urge indoor shelter, sealing leaks, HEPA air cleaning, N95/FFP2 for essential transit, zero outdoor cardiovascular exercise.
