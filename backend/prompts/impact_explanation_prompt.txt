# IMPACT EXPLANATION PROMPT: Multi-Tier Impact Breakdown Framework
# Guides generation of deep, actionable, structured impact explanations

When providing an in-depth impact explanation for any recommended habit, routine change, or sustainable alternative, you MUST structure your analysis across these six rigorous dimensions:

1. RECOMMENDED ACTION:
   - What the user or community should do in precise, tangible steps.
   - Equipment or behavioral shifts required.

2. WHY IT MATTERS (SCIENTIFIC MECHANISM):
   - The underlying physics, chemistry, or atmospheric biology.
   - How the action interrupts the transmission or formation of toxic airborne agents (e.g., PM2.5, NO2, O3, black carbon, polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons).

3. HOW IT HELPS: EXPOSURE VS EMISSION MECHANISM:
   - Must explicitly classify and detail the primary mechanism:
     * EXPOSURE REDUCTION: Shielding the human respiratory and cardiovascular systems from breathing already-polluted ambient or indoor air.
     * EMISSION REDUCTION: Stopping or decreasing the initial release of combustion by-products, volatile organic compounds, or fugitive dust into the shared airshed.
     * DUAL BENEFIT: Simultaneously lowering personal inhalation while diminishing community tailpipe/chimney output.

4. EXPECTED QUALITATIVE IMPACT:
   - Qualitative classification: Low, Medium, or High.
   - Explain why: (e.g., "High: Certified N95 filtration prevents up to 95% of fine particles from penetrating terminal alveoli during heavy outdoor smog").
   - Explicitly avoid inventing unsupported statistical percentages or fabricated epidemiological metrics.

5. DIFFICULTY & ADOPTION FEASIBILITY:
   - Classification: Easy, Moderate, or Difficult.
   - Actionable tips to overcome common barriers (cost, habit friction, inconvenience).

6. SPHERE OF BENEFIT (PERSONAL, HOUSEHOLD, COMMUNITY):
   - Personal Level: Direct individual physiological protection.
   - Household Level: Health and indoor comfort for family members, children, and elderly dependents.
   - Community Level: Collective airshed preservation, traffic decongestion, and neighborhood air improvement.
